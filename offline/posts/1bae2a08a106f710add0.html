Exchange Update: DeFiChain (DFI) at Risk of Delisting from Gate.io

2025-05-28


The DeFiChain team has received notice from Gate.io regarding potential delisting of $DFI due to market liquidity concerns. We want to provide the community with transparent information about this situation and outline our response plan.


What's Happening

Gate.io has identified two specific issues with the $DFI coin:

  • Insufficient market depth: The order book for DFI/USDT trading pairs lacks adequate depth.
  • Significant bid-ask spread: The wide gap between buy and sell orders is negatively affecting trading experience.

Gate.io has requested that these issues be addressed within 3 calendar days, or they may initiate the delisting process as part of their platform-wide risk management standards.


Our Response Plan

The DeFiChain team is taking this matter seriously and has already begun implementing solutions:

  1. Immediate engagement with Gate.io: We've established direct communication with the exchange to understand their specific requirements for market depth and spread improvement.
  2. Liquidity enhancement measures: We're working with market makers to strengthen DFI order books and tighten spreads on Gate.io.
  3. Community market support: We're calling on community members who trade on Gate.io to consider placing limit orders rather than market orders when possible, which helps improve order book depth.


What This Means For $DFI Holders

If you currently hold DFI on Gate.io, we recommend the following precautionary measures:

  • Consider withdrawing to self-custody: If you're holding $DFI for the long term, moving to a personal wallet ensures access regardless of exchange policies.
  • Monitor the situation: We will provide regular updates on our progress with Gate.io through official channels.
  • No immediate action required: At this stage, trading and withdrawals remain available while we work to address the issues.

Stay Informed

We understand this news may cause concern among community members. We are committed to transparency throughout this process and will provide updates through our official channels.

The DeFiChain team remains dedicated to the long-term success of the project and is working diligently to address these market challenges. We appreciate the community's continued support during this time.
