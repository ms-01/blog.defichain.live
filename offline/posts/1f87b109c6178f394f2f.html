Revised Schedule of CFP/DFIP

2023-02-09


There are some slight revisions to the schedule for CFP/DFIP. The previous schedule can be found here. The latest revised schedule for CFP/DFIP can be found in the table below:


Table 1: Schedule of CFP/DFIP

DFIP/CFP Round: First round CFP/DFIP 

  • Proposal Ingestion Period: 26 Jan 2023 (BH: 2620000) to 10 Feb 2023 (BH: 2661120)
  • Valid Voting Period: 26 Jan 2023 (BH: 2620000) Till 24 Feb 2023 (BH: 2701440)

DFIP/CFP Round: Second round CFP/DFIP 

  • Proposal Ingestion Period: 10 Feb 2023 (BH: 2661120) to 6 Mar 2023 (BH: 2730000)
  • Valid Voting Period: 10 Feb 2023 (BH: 2661120) Till 20 Apr 2023 (BH: 2860000)

DFIP/CFP Round: Third round CFP/DFIP onwards

  • Proposal Ingestion Period: 6 Mar 2023 (BH: 2730000) to 20 Apr 2023 (BH: 2860000)
  • Valid Voting Period: 6 Mar 2023 (BH: 2730000) Till 4 June 2023 (BH: 2990000)

It is important to note that the valid voting period is from the moment the proposal is created until the end of the voting period. However, the proposal must be submitted before the end of the proposal ingestion period to be tallied as that round’s proposal. Submissions later than the proposal ingestion period will be counted for the next round’s CFP even though the community can start voting on the proposal already.


Reason for Irregular Duration of CFP/DFIP Rounds

The gap between the introduction of On-Chain Governance and the most recent CFP/DFIP without On-Chain Governance was more than 2 months. To compensate for this, the first round of CFP/DFIP was intentionally made shorter (81440 blocks) to allow the pent up proposals from that 2 month period to be quickly voted on. However, to shorten the first cycle of CFP/DFIP, the second cycle had to be adjusted due to the technical design of On-Chain Governance. This is the reason why the first and second CFP/DFIP rounds have irregular durations.

Thereafter, from the third round of CFP/DFIP onwards, the proposal submission and the valid voting period will always be fixed at 260,000 blocks (approximately 90 days).
