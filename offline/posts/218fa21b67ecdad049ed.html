DeFiChain News Show: Tokenomics, Website, And More!

2025-06-25


Welcome to the DeFiChain News Show summary - your go-to source for all the latest happenings in the DeFiChain ecosystem.

Each week, we bring you the most important updates, developments, and critical announcements that every DeFiChain community member needs to know.

In this edition, we cover the ongoing tokenomics discussion, the revamp of the website, and Crypto Factor's expansion with a dedicated Labs division.



Community Leads Tokenomics Discussion

The DeFiChain community has initiated constructive discussions around tokenomics improvements in a collaborative chat called "Future." This open forum allows all community members to participate directly in shaping the project's economic framework.


Key Discussion Points:

  • Activation of decentralized real-world asset systems
  • Stability fund implementation to maintain DUSD pricing near $1
  • Dynamic interest rate mechanisms for supply and demand balance
  • Reduction of interest rates from 5% to 0.5% to encourage borrowing

The community has published a draft document titled "DeFiChain Tokenomics Discussion" that outlines proposed changes to the current system. These discussions represent a shift toward community-driven decision-making rather than waiting for top-down initiatives.


Website Modernization in Progress

DeFiChain is prioritizing website updates to better serve both desktop and mobile users. The development team is working with DeFiChain Labs to create an enhanced website experience for 2025.

The updated site aims to make DeFiChain more accessible to newcomers while providing comprehensive information about the platform's capabilities and ongoing developments.


Crypto Factor Expands with Dedicated Labs Division

The Crypto Factor project has announced a significant organizational restructuring to better serve the expanding DeFiChain ecosystem:

  • Crypto Factor Labs: New division focused on research, development, and technical components
  • Core Crypto Factor Team: Will concentrate on adoption, onboarding, and ecosystem expansion

This separation allows for more specialized focus on both technical innovation and user growth within the Metachain ecosystem.


DFIPVoting Now Active

The community can now vote on a new DFIP, which proposes the listing of new wrapped crypto tokens on the DeFiChain network. Voting remains open until July 15, 2025.

This proposal represents another step in expanding DeFiChain's asset offerings and improving platform utility. Community members are encouraged to review the detailed proposal documentation before casting their votes through the on-chain governance system.
