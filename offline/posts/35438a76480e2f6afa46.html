📢 Introduction to the Marketing SIG

2025-09-12


As DeFiChain begins a new era of decentralization where the community directly runs 100% of the project, we, the Marketing Special Interest Group (SIG), are pleased to introduce ourselves to the public.

Our team is dedicated to strengthening the public image of DeFiChain, providing updates to the rest of the community, and promoting DeFiChain to a broader audience.

This post discusses:

  • Who we are
  • What we’re working on
  • Goals for the rest of the year


👥 Who We Are

Just like you, we value transparency. We want to know who’s involved, be informed about who’s on the team, and be able to reach out to them. The SIG consists of core members, who started the group, as well as extended members, who provide support for the core members. In the linked spreadsheet, you can learn more about our 6 core members and 4 extended members:

https://tinyurl.com/marketingsig202509

In addition, the Marketing SIG can be reached at

marketingsigdefichain@protonmail.com, our temporary email address.


💼 What We’re Doing

Transparency also means sharing detailed information about our activities. After the departure of DeFiChain Labs, this means that the Marketing SIG is wholly responsible for the following tasks and platforms:

  • Operating DeFiChain’s official website (https://defichain.com) in coordination with our two website developers, who have asked to remain anonymous outside of the SIG to retain their privacy.
  • Managing DeFiChain’s official Telegram and YouTube channels (a list of them can be found at https://tinyurl.com/socialmediacontactlistsheet, along with the community members in charge of moderation).
  • Managing DeFiChain’s official subreddit (https://www.reddit.com/r/defiblockchain/)
  • Operating DeFiChain’s official X account (https://x.com/defichain)
  • Operating DeFiChain’s official blog.


Make sure to SUBSCRIBE to the new blog!

We’ll provide critical updates there. It takes less than a minute! Click the three bars on the top left of the blog and enter your email. This new blog will save DeFiChain 380 USD per year.


🏆 Q3/Q4 Goals

In the coming weeks and months, these are the objectives that the Marketing SIG hopes to achieve.

  • Collaborate with DeFiChain Labs to wrap up the takeover.
  • Create the official , permanent Marketing SIG email for more professionalism and organization.
  • Transfer ownership of social media channels to the DeFiChain Empowerment Account to enable greater decentralization.
  • Consider the idea of starting an airdrop campaign after the community thoroughly analyzes the tokenomics of DeFiChain’s dAsset system.
  • Encourage more conversation of proposals
    • DFIP – reallocate block rewards
    • DFIP – formation of Development SIG
    • DFIP – change of tokenomics for dAssets

We give a big thanks to you, a member of the DeFiChain community, just like our developers, moderators, other SIG members, and everyone else who volunteers behind the scenes. If you want to take a more active role in improving DeFiChain like us, talk in the chats and you will get to join us. There’s no application needed; this is an open project for everyone.

We look forward to bringing the community together and driving our shared vision forward. We are truly glad to have all of you on this journey with DeFiChain.
