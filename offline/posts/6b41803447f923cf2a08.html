Project Spotlight: Dex Trading Live

2024-07-24


The DeFiChain community is filled with talented builders constantly advancing the ecosystem's capabilities. 

Today we're spotlighting DEX Trading Live - an educational initiative aimed at promoting decentralized trading.

DEX Trading Live is one of the most comprehensive learning resource for trading on DeFiChain’s decentralized exchange (DEX). 

We recently sat down with the founders to learn about their achievements since our last spotlight.


Promoting Decentralized Trading on DeFiChain

DEX Trading Live is a community initiative founded on the principles of decentralized finance and empowering traders through education. 

Its mission is to drive adoption of the DeFiChain DEX as a platform by creating a community of knowledgeable traders who take advantage of the DEX for trading dTokens. 

Through educational initiatives and an engaging social media presence, the project seeks to find more traders to explore the possibilities of the DeFiChain DEX.

By creating a collaborative community focused on dToken trading, DEX Trading Live aspires to make the DeFiChain DEX a go-to destination for decentralized finance. 

With the right information and support network, traders can unlock the full potential of trading directly on the blockchain through DeFiChain.
