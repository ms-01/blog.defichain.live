The Birth of DeFiChain Labs: Setting the Stage for MetaChain Mainnet

With the MetaChain layer on the horizon, it's vital to optimize communication, increase community interaction, and bolster innovative endeavors. DeFiChain Labs stands as a beacon of progress, championing transparency, collaboration, and collective growth.


A Seamless Transition: Uniting the Past and the Future

DeFiChain Labs inherits the mantle of the DeFiChain Accelerator team, aiming to further its marketing strategy by magnifying its outreach and streamlining communications as well as building out the core layer from a tech perspective. In essence, DeFiChain Labs will turn the key to unlock DeFiChain’s potential.

Collaboration: The core ethos of DeFiChain Labs is centered around collective effort and transparent dialogue, fostering a platform where both staff and community merge their strengths to advance the project’s goals..

Understanding the Distinct Entities: DeFiChain Labs, DeFiChain Foundation & Birthday Research

DeFiChain Labs joins the DeFiChain family as one of its three core pillars. Below is an overview of DeFiChain’s pillars and their roles:


DeFiChain Labs

DeFiChain Labs is an independent, open-source team that focuses on innovation.

Born from the union of core contributing teams to DeFiChain, DeFiChain Labs builds growth strategies, organizes ecosystem-enhancing initiatives like hackathons and collaborations, and manages blockchain engineering for the DeFiChain and MetaChain layers.


DeFiChain Foundation

Formerly a core contributing team, the DeFiChain Foundation has transformed.

Its predecessor, DeFiChain Foundation Limited, has been deregistered. In its place, a non-profit foundation was established in the BVI, prioritizing administrative tasks and protecting DeFiChain's intellectual property rights.

Daniel Zirkel (DZ) is the intermediary and communicator between DeFiChain’s new Foundation and its community.


Birthday Research

As Cake Group's dedicated R&D arm, Birthday Research builds decentralized apps (dApps) and protocols on the DeFiChain network. Birthday Research is the brain behind key products like the Light Wallet and Quantum Bridge.
