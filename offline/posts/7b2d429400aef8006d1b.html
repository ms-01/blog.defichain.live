DeFiChain Labs Tech Sync – November 2024

2024-12-02


The DeFiChain Labs team recently held their monthly Tech Sync, offering valuable insights into ongoing projects and upcoming network enhancements. 

Here’s a summary of the updates:


Key Achievements in 2024

The November DeFiChain Tech Sync underscored the team’s commitment to decentralization and performance improvements. This year, the roadmap spanned from the EVM integration to governance upgrades, setting a strong foundation for future advancements.


EVM Integration and Enhancements

  • The year kicked off with enhancements to the EVM integration launched in 2023.
  • Addressed pressing issues like RPC fixes and blockscout resolutions, ensuring smoother operations.
  • Introduced tracing and subscription APIs to bolster developer tooling and network visibility.
  • Rolled out transformative features such as token splits, merges, and renames, empowering greater flexibility.


Improved Token Management

  • Support for fractional splits and token ownership transfers streamlined operations.
  • A feature to rename tokens—critical for adapting to industry rebrands—added flexibility.


Stability in DVM Operations

  • Incremental flow of time was added to enhance network stability and predictability.
  • Introduced static rewards during token splits, significantly reducing the complexity of operational preparation.
  • These changes collectively streamlined token management, making splits less labor-intensive.


On-Chain Governance and Automation

  • Automated CFP payouts and future swap limits have been set for activation, heralding a new era of efficiency.
  • Progress continues toward full decentralization with robust governance frameworks and transparent decision-making processes.


Infrastructure Optimization

  • Integrated Ocean API into nodes, slashing indexing times from weeks to mere hours, drastically improving efficiency.
  • Advancements in containerization and dependency decoupling simplified infrastructure management, paving the way for community-led operations.


Community Contributions

  • A noteworthy highlight was the community-driven token restart initiative, showcasing collaboration between developers and stakeholders.
  • Such initiatives underline the shift toward a truly decentralized ecosystem.


Governance: The Road Ahead

Governance has taken center stage in DeFiChain’s decentralization roadmap. The governance repo, set to be fully operational by year-end, will host all decision-making processes transparently.

Key next steps include:

  • Bootstrapping Governance SIGs: Initial groups will define charters, processes, and rotation policies.
  • Clear Task Management: Governance SIGs, like the Economy SIG, will oversee parameters such as interest rates or fees.
  • Execution Protocols: Acknowledgment-based execution ensures transparency and prevents unilateral decisions.


Challenges and Opportunities

Despite the progress, certain aspects remain incomplete. For example, parameterized time adjustments to speed up network activity stand at 90% completion. These unfinished tasks provide opportunities for community members to contribute in 2025.

The ongoing transition to decentralization requires proactive participation. While governance structures are being established, their success hinges on collective ownership and collaboration. Community members are encouraged to actively engage, propose ideas, and lead implementations.


Closing Thoughts

DeFiChain’s 2024 journey has been one of transformation, laying the groundwork for a more resilient and decentralized future. As the year closes, the focus shifts toward empowering the community to take the reins. The advancements made this year not only bolster the ecosystem’s technical capabilities but also signal a commitment to collaborative growth.

The success of these efforts will depend on the collective enthusiasm and ingenuity of the DeFiChain community. Whether you're a developer, a node operator, or a stakeholder, your contributions will help shape the future of decentralized finance.
