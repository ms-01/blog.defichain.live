Trade DFI to Earn Your Share of $15,000 in DFI Tokens! 🚀

2020-11-05


To celebrate the successful BTC-DFI and USDT-DFI market launch on October 16th, 2020, Bittrex Global is giving away $15,000 in value through a 7-day trading competition!

Read below to find out the multiple ways that you could get involved!

Period for all promotions:

Start date: November 2nd 09:00:00 PST

End date: November 9th 09:00:00 PST

Markets: BTC-DFI and USDT-DFI


Promotion #1: Buy DFI to Win $7,500 worth of DFI Tokens

The top 50 accounts with the largest amount of trading volume (buying + selling amounts) will earn a share of $7,500 in DFI Tokens.

If you’re in the top 50 trading accounts, your reward amount will be proportional to the total volume you contributed to the aggregate volume of the top 50 accounts.

Reward for each in the top 50:

  • $7,500 in DFI * (Individual Account volume / Sum of top 50 accounts’ volume)


Promotion #2: Buy DFI to Win $5,000 in DFI Tokens

The top 20 accounts with the highest net amount of DFI (buying amount — selling amount — withdrawal amount) will earn a share of $5,000 in DFI Tokens.

Additional eligibility requirements:

  • 1st-3rd place, users must purchase a minimum of USD $2,500 worth of DFI.
  • 4th-20th place, users must purchase a minimum of USD $250 worth of DFI.

Rewards:

  • 1st place: $2,000 worth of DFI Tokens
  • 2nd place: $1,000 worth of DFI Tokens
  • 3rd place: $500 worth of DFI Tokens
  • 4th-20th place: Equally split prize pool of $1,500 worth of DFI Tokens


Promotion #3: Sign up for a Bittrex Global account, trade DFI, and get free tokens

Get $10 worth of DFI tokens when you sign up for Bittrex Global & make either a BTC-DFI or USDT-DFI Trade!

New to Bittrex Global? The first 250 new Bittrex Global traders will receive $10 worth of DFI tokens for FREE when they make either a BTC-DFI or USDT-DFI trade!


How It Works:

Deposit or purchase BTC or USDT into your new Bittrex Global account and participate in the DFI trading competition by purchasing DFI tokens between the trading competition start and end dates.

Trades with your new Bittrex Global account must be purchasing DFI tokens to qualify. Once the competition is finished on the end date, we’ll deposit an additional $10 DFI tokens into your account.

New Bittrex Global account users are all new accounts opened 30 days prior to the start date


Terms:

Bittrex Global reserves the right to disqualify trades that are deemed to be illegal bulk registration account, self-dealing or display attributes of market manipulation, etc.

Rewards will be distributed within 2–3 weeks after the competition ends.

Bittrex Global reserves the right to cancel or amend any Activity or Activity Rules at our sole discretion.

Happy trading & enjoy your rewards! :-)
