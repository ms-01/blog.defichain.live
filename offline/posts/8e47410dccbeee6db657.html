DeFiChain Anniversary Celebration: $50,000 in DFI to Give Away!

2021-05-11


To celebrate DeFiChain’s (DFI) anniversary, KuCoin has launched a celebration campaign to give away a reward pool of $50,000 in DFI to qualified KuCoin users.

Learn more about DeFiChain (DFI): https://defichain.com

Register for KuCoin here: https://kucoin.com

The campaign will run from 10:00:00 on May 11, 2021 to 10:00:00 on May 18, 2021 (UTC).


Activity 1: Net Buying Competition, Enjoy a $25,000 in DFI Prize Pool!

The top 20 accounts with the highest net buying volume (buys - sells - withdrawals) of the DFI/USDT and DFI/BTC trading pairs on KuCoin will win a share of $25,000 in DFI!

The rewards will be distributed as follows:



Activity 2: Holding Competition, Enjoy a $15,000 in DFI Prize Pool!

During the campaign period, KuCoin will take a snapshot of each user’s DFI balance each day at 16:00 (UTC). The top 20 accounts with the highest average holding amount of DFI per day on KuCoin will win a share of a $15,000 in DFI prize pool.

The reward will be distributed as follows:



*Snapshot Period: Every 16:00:00 from May 11, 2021 to May 17, 2021 (UTC)


Activity 3: Double Participation Rewards: Win a Share of a $10,000 in DFI Prize Pool!

  1. Users who have a DFI net buying volume (buys - sells - withdrawals) of 40 DFI or more on KuCoin and add the DFI/USDT and DFI/BTC trading pairs to their Favorites List will evenly split a $4,000 in DFI prize pool.
  2. Users who have a DFI net buying volume (buys - sells - withdrawals) of 60 DFI or more on KuCoin and add the DFI/USDT and DFI/BTC trading pairs to their Favorites List will evenly split a $6,000 in DFI prize pool.

*Qualified users can receive double participation rewards.


Note:

  1. Net Buying Volume: buys - sells - withdrawals;
  2. The quantity of rewards will be calculated based on the average closing price of DFI/USDT during the campaign period;
  3. The Sub-Accounts and Master Account will be treated as the same account when participating in the DFI activity;
  4. Top 20 users of Activity 1 & 2 will not be eligible to receive the participation reward (Activity 3);
  5. KuCoin will update the top 10 rankings of Activity 1 & 2 before 13:00 on May 14, 2021 (UTC). The final rankings will be updated within 5 working days of the campaign ending;
  6. Rewards will be distributed within 7 working days of the campaign ending;
  7. The trading activity on the platform will undergo rigorous inspection during the activity. For any malicious acts conducted during the period, including malicious transaction manipulations, illegal bulk registration of accounts, self-dealing, etc., the platform will cancel the qualification of the participants. KuCoin reserves all rights to exercise at its sole discretion to define if the transaction behavior is counted as cheating behavior and determine whether to cancel the participation qualification of a user. The final decision made by KuCoin is with legal binding force to all participants who participated in the competition. Users hereby confirm that their registration and use of KuCoin is voluntary and is not forced, interfered with, or influenced by KuCoin in any way.

Register for KuCoin here: https://kucoin.com

For questions & discussions, join the official DeFiChain Reddit community.
