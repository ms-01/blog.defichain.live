Big Things Have Happened. Bigger Things Are Coming.

2021-11-06


With the festive season around us and the year about to come to an end, it’s time to reflect on all the great milestones the DeFiChain engineering team, together with our unique community, have managed to achieve. Without their relentless dedication towards building mind-blowing products and services, DeFiChain would not be here where it is today.

November 6th marks a very special day in DeFiChain’s history as it is the second anniversary of the whitepaper publication. What once used to be a compendium of abstract concepts and ideas has evolved into a tangible outcome –– real products that are used by thousands of people worldwide. And the best part is: it’s totally decentralized and truly everyone with just an internet connection is able to use the amazing products built on the world’s first #NativeDeFi blockchain.

Just recently, we announced one of the most anticipated updates in recent history. The Fort Canning update will bring new exciting products and services to DeFiChain. Decentralized stock trading is about to hit the DeFiChain ecosystem with a big bang at block height 1,367,000, which is around November 15th. Considering the rapid increase in new master nodes and the influx of new people into our social media channels, the whole DeFiChain community is looking forward to this special date full of joy.

A first indication of that is the increase in the DFI price. And oh boy, did $DFI skyrocket over the last couple of days –– well on its way to a new all time high. Yet one thing is clear, without our community this wouldn’t have been possible in the first place. The community is our backbone when it comes to delivering our core messages around social media channels as well as to help onboarding new customers. We are truly grateful to have such a vibrant, dedicated and welcoming community. We simply love you!

To wrap things up, we would like to reflect on our whitepaper and the exciting development work of the past 2 years. The following infographic should give you an idea how far our ecosystem has evolved over such a short period of time and we are looking forward to many more revolutionary years to come!
