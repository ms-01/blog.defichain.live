DeFiChain x Bittrex Airdrop

2022-08-06


In celebration of DeFiChain's partnership with Bittrex, Bittrex has announced an AirDrop to earn 22 600 $DFI tokens. You can enter the AirDrop by visiting: https://gleam.io/vYoif/defichain-dfi-airdrop

For a chance to win a slice of the pie, you must complete the following tasks by 9 August 2022 9am PDT. As you complete more tasks, your chances of receiving $DFI increase.

  • Provide your verified Bittrex Global email address
  • Follow @DeFiChain on Twitter
  • Follow @BittrexGlobal on Twitter
  • Join the DeFiChain Discord server
  • Join the DeFiChain Discord server
  • Join @defiblockchain on Telegram
  • Retweet!

You can find the T&Cs directly on the promotion page.
