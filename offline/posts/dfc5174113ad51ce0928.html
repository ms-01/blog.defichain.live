CFP Approved: Strengthening Bitrue Market Making

The DeFiChain community has approved the CFP "Strengthening Bitrue Market Making to Secure CEX Listing" with the following results:

• 👍 1,223 votes (73.06%)
• 👎 451 votes (26.94%)
• Total votes: 1,674

Thank you to everyone who participated in the vote and contributed to the discussion. Community feedback, both supportive and critical, plays an important role in the governance process.

The approved funding is intended to support and strengthen market-making activities on Bitrue, helping maintain and improve DeFiChain's centralized exchange presence.

With Community Fund inflows continuing through block rewards as defined by an approved DFIP, the approved amount is expected to be replenished within approx 4.5 Months under current conditions.

The Marketing SIG looks forward to following the progress of this initiative and sharing future updates with the community.

Telegram Channel Consolidation

Over the past few weeks, we have retired several of our country-specific Telegram channels, including Italy, India, Turkey, Spain, France, and Portugal.

The goal is to consolidate community activity, discussions, and important updates into our two main Telegram channels:

English Community:
https://t.me/defiblockchain

German Community:
https://t.me/defiblockchain_DE

Going forward, all major announcements, reports, and ecosystem updates will be shared through these channels, making it easier for the community to stay informed and engaged without information being spread across multiple groups.

We look forward to seeing you there.

dUSDC Update

The introduction of native dUSDC remains one of the most anticipated developments within the DeFiChain ecosystem.

While there has not yet been a new official update from the DTL team, development work on the project is understood to be ongoing. The community is currently awaiting further information regarding progress, remaining milestones, and the expected next steps.

As previously communicated by DTL, development resources were temporarily focused on the expansion of the DTL ecosystem to Polygon. Once an official progress update becomes available, we will share it with the community through our usual communication channels.

Native dUSDC is expected to play an important role in strengthening liquidity, improving user experience, and supporting future ecosystem growth on DeFiChain.

We appreciate the community's continued patience and look forward to sharing more information as soon as an official update is released.
